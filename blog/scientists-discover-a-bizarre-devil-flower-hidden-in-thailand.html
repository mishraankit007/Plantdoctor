Scientists discover a bizarre “devil flower” hidden in Thailand

Plant Doctor Team · Curated from Science News · September 03, 2026

Scientists have discovered a strange black “devil flower” in Thailand that lives mostly underground, steals nutrients from fungi, and looks strikingly unlike a typical plant. The newly named Thismia daemona is known from fewer than 50 individuals and may already be critically endangered.

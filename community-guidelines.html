Space Ranking — Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 5, 2026

The Space Ranking leaderboard is a public feature — anyone using the app or visiting this website can view submitted photos, display names, and scores. By submitting a space, you agree to these guidelines.

1. What you can submit

Only submit a real photo of your own garden, terrace, balcony, or indoor plant space that you have the right to share. Each submission is scored by AI and ranked publicly against other users' spaces.

2. Prohibited content

Do not submit content that:

  • Is not actually a garden, terrace, balcony, or indoor plant space
  • Contains people (especially children), faces, or personally identifying details you don't want public
  • Is illegal, abusive, hateful, sexually explicit, or otherwise objectionable
  • Infringes someone else's copyright or privacy (for example, a photo you didn't take or don't have permission to share)
  • Is spam, an advertisement, or unrelated to plants and gardening

3. Reporting and blocking

If you see an entry that violates these guidelines, use the Report this entry button on that entry's page in the app. Entries are automatically hidden once they receive multiple reports, pending review. You can also block a specific user directly from their entry — once blocked, you won't see any of their future submissions.

4. Enforcement

We review reported content and may permanently remove any entry, and suspend the ability to submit to the leaderboard for any account, that violates these guidelines — without prior notice.

5. Contact

To report a serious issue directly, or if you believe your own content was removed in error, contact us at:

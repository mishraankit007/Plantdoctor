Privacy Policy for Plant Doctor

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plant Doctor ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address or phone number (whichever you sign up with), and, if you sign in with Google, your name and email address from your Google account. We also store your selected role (customer or nursery) and display name.

Plant photos and analysis data. Photos you take or upload of plants, planting spaces, or farm plots are sent to our AI provider for analysis (see Section 3) and are stored in our cloud database, linked to your account, so you can view your plant history and care reminders.

Location data. With your permission, we access your device's approximate or precise location to: (a) suggest plants suited to your local climate, (b) generate daily farming/gardening tips relevant to your area, and (c) auto-fill your region in the Farm Advisor tool. Location is used to generate a place name (e.g. "city, region, country") and is not stored on our servers — it is used only for the specific request being made and cached temporarily on your own device.

Chat messages. If you use the "Ask about this plant/plan" chat feature, your messages (text, voice recordings, or photos/videos you attach) are sent to our AI provider to generate a response. Chat history is stored locally on your device only — we do not store your chat conversations on our servers.

Voice recordings. If you send a voice message in chat, the recording is processed to generate a response and is not permanently stored by us beyond what's needed to complete that request.

Usage and device data. We may collect basic technical information such as app version and device type to diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Identify plants and assess their health from photos
  • Generate personalized care schedules and reminders
  • Provide farming and planting-space advice
  • Show location-relevant tips and climate context
  • Answer your follow-up questions via the in-app chat
  • Maintain your account and plant history across sessions
  • Send local reminder notifications (watering, sunlight, health follow-ups, farm risk alerts) — these are scheduled and delivered entirely on your own device; we do not use a remote push notification service
  • Enforce free-tier and subscription limits
  • Provide customer support

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third parties, only as needed to provide the app's features:

  • Google Gemini API — plant/farm photos, text descriptions, and chat messages you submit are sent to Google's Gemini API for AI analysis. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — our backend provider, used for authentication, database storage (your profile and plant records), and photo storage. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled by Google OAuth.
  • SMS/WhatsApp OTP provider — if you sign in with a phone number, your phone number is shared with our messaging provider solely to deliver a one-time verification code.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information and plant records for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual plants at any time in the app. To request full account deletion and erasure of your associated data, contact us at the email below — we will process deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Plant Doctor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
